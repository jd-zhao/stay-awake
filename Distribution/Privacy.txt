Stay Awake — Privacy

The app runs locally and does not make network requests, collect analytics, create accounts or send your information to a server.

Timer, mode, appearance and menu bar preferences are stored locally in macOS UserDefaults. Optional login registration is managed by macOS ServiceManagement.

The app reads console lock/active-user state to pause screen protection while locked or switched away. It does not record or transmit that state. During a Screen and Mac session, it reports user activity without generating keyboard or mouse events.

It does not request microphone, camera, contacts, documents, screen recording or Accessibility access.

Documentation renderers and diagnostics use isolated temporary preferences. Developer notarization tooling submits the app binary to Apple only when explicitly run; the installed app does not do so.
