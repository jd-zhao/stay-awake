Stay Awake
Native wake timers for macOS.

A compact menu bar app for keeping your Mac awake for a chosen duration or until a specific time.

• Keep the screen and Mac awake, or allow the screen to sleep while tasks run.
• Choose a preset, enter hours and minutes, or select an end time.
• Follow the system's light/dark appearance.
• Optionally show the countdown in the menu bar and open at login.
• Runs locally, with no account, advertising, analytics or network requests.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel.
Manual lock, lid closure and managed-device policies can still apply.
